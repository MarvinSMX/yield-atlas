Why Choose CHPY?

✅ Weekly Income Potential

CHPY distributes income on a weekly basis, providing consistent cash flow every single week instead of monthly or quarterly.

✅ Diversified Semiconductor Exposure

Gain exposure to 15–30 publicly traded semiconductor companies, providing diversification while maintaining focus on a high-growth sector.

✅ Capital Appreciation Potential

While harvesting option income, CHPY maintains direct equity exposure, allowing investors to benefit from semiconductor stock price appreciation.

⚠️ Important Risk Disclosure

Capped Upside: If semiconductor stocks rally significantly, your upside is limited because the fund has sold call options.

Uncapped Downside: If semiconductor stocks decline, you experience the full downside loss — not offset by income unless held.

Return of Capital: Distributions may include return of investor capital, which can decrease NAV over time and impact long-term returns.

No Guarantee: There is no guarantee the fund will achieve its objectives or maintain distribution levels.
