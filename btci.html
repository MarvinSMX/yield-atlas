BTCI Investment Strategy

Objective: BTCI seeks to generate high monthly income with potential appreciation based on exposure to Bitcoin exchange-traded products (ETPs).

Strategy: The Fund implements a data-driven call option writing strategy on Bitcoin Futures ETFs, allowing investors to benefit from Bitcoin exposure while generating premium income. Distributions may consist of option premiums, dividends, capital gains, and interest payments.

Benefit: Actively managed by NEOS, BTCI offers efficient exposure to Bitcoin while the team handles the complexities of options trading.
